About MetaCleaner

Every photo you capture, office document you save, or audio file you export embeds hidden identification attributes known as metadata. While intended for cataloging, this hidden data frequently compromises personal security and privacy.

What is Metadata?

Metadata is "data about data," embedded invisibly inside file containers:

  • Photos: GPS latitude/longitude, camera model, lens aperture, serial numbers, timestamps.
  • Documents: Author names, company identities, revision numbers, template paths, editing durations.
  • Media: Encoders, comments, performer names, copyright strings.

Why Clean Metadata?

Sharing an uncleaned photo or file publicly or with third parties exposes your exact location, equipment, software versions, and identity. MetaCleaner strips away all tracking traces while preserving visual and functional fidelity.

Engine Architecture

  • Images: Pillow engine with EXIF orientation auto-transposition, saving pure pixel streams with purged metadata chunks.
  • PDFs: pypdf page rebuilding with full XML/XMP stream deletion.
  • Office: Zip stream parser neutralizing docProps/core.xml, docProps/custom.xml, and docProps/app.xml.
  • Audio/Video: Mutagen and FFmpeg container sanitization.
