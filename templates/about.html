About MetaCleaner

A high-performance, zero-storage privacy utility to permanently purge tracking signatures, EXIF tags, and hidden metadata from your files.

What is Metadata?

Metadata is "data about data," embedded invisibly inside your files during creation or export. Modern cameras, smartphones, office suites, and editors silently record extensive personal identifiers.

Photos & Images

Precise GPS coordinates, camera model, lens aperture, serial numbers, timestamps, and editor signatures.

Documents & Sheets

Author names, corporate accounts, revision histories, editing duration, and network file paths.

Media & Code

Audio/video encoders, hidden watermarks, zero-width tracking characters, and performer details.

Why Remove Metadata?

Sharing an uncleaned photo or document exposes your home location, organizational hierarchy, software versions, and identity to anyone with a basic file inspector. MetaCleaner strips away all tracking traces while preserving 100% of your visual and functional content.

Core Engine Architecture

  • Images & Vectors: Pillow engine with EXIF orientation auto-transposition, saving pure pixel streams without metadata chunks (JPEG, PNG, WEBP, TIFF, HEIC, SVG).
  • Documents & Spreadsheets: Byte-level OpenXML package parser purging docProps/core.xml, custom.xml, and app.xml (DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, ODT, ODS).
  • PDF Documents: pypdf page rebuilder with complete catalog-level XML/XMP stream deletion.
  • Text & Markdown: Zero-width tracking watermark detection and strip engine, removing invisible leak-tracking characters.
  • Audio & Video: Stream-copy container sanitizers dropping tag containers (MP3, MP4, WAV, FLAC, MKV).
