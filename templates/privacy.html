Zero-Trust Architecture

Privacy Policy

Your data belongs to you alone. MetaCleaner is built on an uncompromising zero-storage, zero-retention privacy standard.

1. Zero Storage Policy

Uploaded files are handled purely in ephemeral memory and transient temporary directories. No files are indexed, categorized, archived, or permanently saved on server disks.

2. Immediate Destruction on Download

The moment you download your cleaned file, the server executes an immediate low-level deletion sequence that purges both the source upload and the cleaned copy from disk storage.

3. Automated Pruning Sweep

Any temporary files that are uploaded but never downloaded are automatically wiped by background sweepers after 15 minutes of inactivity.

4. No User Tracking or Accounts

There are no registration portals, tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or database records logging your IP address or session details.
