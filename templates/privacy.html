Zero-Trust Architecture

Privacy Policy

Your data belongs to you alone. MetaCleaner is built on an absolute zero-storage, zero-retention foundation.

01 / POLICY

Zero Storage

Uploaded files are handled strictly in volatile ephemeral memory and transient system directories. No file is ever indexed, stored, or archived to permanent disks.

02 / PURGE

Immediate Destruction

The moment you download your cleaned file, the server runs an automatic low-level deletion routine that permanently purges both the upload and the cleaned copy.

03 / SWEEPER

15-Minute Auto Sweep

Any temporary files from abandoned or interrupted browser sessions are automatically detected and erased by server background sweepers every 15 minutes.

04 / ANONYMOUS

Zero Tracking & Logs

MetaCleaner requires no logins, accounts, or profiles. We use zero tracking cookies, zero analytics pixels, and keep zero IP address history.
